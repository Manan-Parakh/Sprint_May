Overview

The Production Stack

Two tools, one pipeline. ElevenLabs handles voice; HeyGen handles the avatar and lip-sync. Keeping them separate avoids HeyGen's accent drift issue on cloned voices.

Why this order matters

HeyGen can generate its own voice, but cloned voices drift toward an American inflection. By generating audio in ElevenLabs first and uploading it to HeyGen, the avatar lip-syncs to your audio — the Indian English accent stays locked exactly as recorded. Never use HeyGen's built-in voice clone for Indian English.

🎙️ ElevenLabs — what you use it for

  • Clone a branded Indian English voice (one-time setup)
  • Generate voiceover audio for each video
  • Fine-tune pace, pauses, and pronunciation
  • Export as MP3 or WAV

🎬 HeyGen — what you use it for

  • Choose or build the on-screen avatar
  • Upload the ElevenLabs audio
  • Let HeyGen lip-sync the avatar to that audio
  • Set background, add captions, export final video
One-time setup

Account & Tool Setup

Do this once before your first production. Takes ~2–3 hours total.

A
ElevenLabs Setup
Account · Voice clone · Voice settings
~60 min
  • 1
    Create ElevenLabs account & subscribe to Creator plan
    Go to elevenlabs.io → Sign up → Upgrade to Creator ($11/mo). This unlocks Professional Voice Cloning, 100,000 credits/mo, and 192 kbps audio quality.
    💡 The free tier (10,000 credits ≈ 10 min) is enough for initial experimentation, but Professional Voice Cloning requires Creator or above.
  • 2
    Record your voice sample for cloning
    Record a clean audio sample of the person whose voice will represent Trulo Foods. ElevenLabs needs 1–5 minutes of high-quality audio. This should be the brand's spokesperson voice — Indian English, conversational-professional tone.
    # Recording spec
    Mic: Any condenser mic or iPhone in a quiet room
    Format: MP3 or WAV, 44.1 kHz, no background noise
    Length: 2–5 minutes of continuous natural speech
    Content: Read a product description or brand story aloud — varied sentences
    Avoid: Music, echo, plosives (use a pop filter or move back from mic)
    ⚠️ Do NOT use music, background sound, or over-processed audio in the training sample. ElevenLabs will clone those artifacts too.
  • 3
    Create the Professional Voice Clone in ElevenLabs
    Voices → Add a new voice → Professional Voice Clone → Upload your recording. Name the voice something like "Trulo — Priya (Indian EN)". Training takes 15–30 min.
    # Recommended voice settings after cloning
    Stability: 55–65 (lower = more expressive, higher = more consistent)
    Similarity boost: 75–80 (how closely it mirrors the clone)
    Style exaggeration: 20–30 (subtle warmth without over-acting)
    Speaker boost: ON (boosts similarity for short texts)
    💡 Save these settings as the default for this voice. You can always adjust per-video, but having a baseline prevents drift across episodes.
  • 4
    Test the voice clone with a 30-second sample script
    Type a product sentence into the ElevenLabs Studio and generate. Listen for: natural Indian English cadence, correct stress patterns, no robotic pauses. Adjust Stability/Style until it sounds right. Save a reference audio file.
    ✅ Pass criteria: The voice sounds like a confident, warm Indian English speaker. Stress on the right syllables. No American vowel shifts.
B
HeyGen Setup
Account · Avatar selection or creation · Brand defaults
~60 min
  • 5
    Create HeyGen account & subscribe to Creator plan
    Go to heygen.com → Sign up → Creator ($24/mo annual). This gives 200 generative credits/mo ≈ 10 min of Avatar IV video, watermark-free exports, and custom avatar creation.
    💡 Use the 3-video free tier first to test your workflow before subscribing.
  • 6
    Choose your avatar approach
    Two options depending on budget and brand intent:

    Option A — Stock Avatar (Faster, cheaper)

    • Browse HeyGen's 1,100+ stock avatars
    • Filter: Female / South Asian appearance / Professional
    • Pick one that feels aligned with Trulo Foods
    • Cost: Included in Creator plan
    • Best for: Starting out, testing formats

    Option B — Custom Avatar (Recommended long-term)

    • Record a 2–5 min "consent video" of the spokesperson
    • Upload to HeyGen Avatar Studio → train custom avatar
    • Creates a digital twin of your actual spokesperson
    • Cost: Included in Creator plan for basic custom avatar
    • Best for: Brand consistency, spokesperson identity
    ⚠️ For a custom avatar, the spokesperson must record a verbal consent statement at the start of the video: "I, [name], consent to HeyGen creating an AI avatar in my likeness for use by Trulo Foods."
  • 7
    Set up a Brand Template in HeyGen
    Create a reusable video template so every video starts with consistent settings — no manual re-setup each time.
    # HeyGen template defaults for Trulo Foods
    Aspect ratio: 9:16 (Reels/Shorts primary)
    Also save: 16:9 (YouTube / website embed)
    Background: Clean gradient (warm cream / off-white) OR brand-branded BG image
    Avatar position: Centre frame, slightly right of middle
    Captions: ON by default — white text, black outline, bottom-third
    Resolution: 1080p
    Brand watermark: Trulo logo top-right (upload once)
Phase 1
1
Pre-Production
Brief → Script → Review → Approve
20–30 min per video
  • 1
    Define the video brief
    Before writing a word, answer these five questions. Keep a brief doc per video.
    Video typeProduct explainer / Social ad / Brand story / Recipe tip / Ingredient spotlight
    PlatformInstagram Reels · YouTube Shorts · YouTube long-form · Website embed
    Target length30 sec · 60 sec · 90 sec · 3 min
    One goalWhat is the single thing the viewer should know/do after watching?
    CTALink in bio / Buy now / DM us / Comment below / Visit trulofoods.com
  • 2
    Write the script using the Trulo template
    Talking head scripts follow a strict structure. Write to time: ~130 words = 60 seconds at a natural Indian English pace.
    HOOK (0–3 sec)1 punchy sentence that stops the scroll. State the problem or bold claim.
    e.g. "Most energy bars are just candy in disguise."
    SETUP (3–10 sec)Who you are / what this video is about. One sentence max.
    VALUE BODY (10–45 sec)The actual content. 2–3 points max. Each point = 1–2 sentences. Simple, conversational language. No jargon.
    PROOF (45–55 sec)One specific, credible detail — an ingredient, a stat, a real outcome. Builds trust.
    CTA (55–60 sec)One clear action. Tie it back to the hook.
    e.g. "Try Trulo — link in bio. Your body will thank you."
    💡 Read the script aloud and time it. If it feels rushed, cut — don't speed up. The avatar will pace naturally at ~130 wpm. Also add pronunciation guides for any unusual product names: [troo-loh].
  • 3
    Script review and sign-off
    One person reviews for: factual accuracy, brand tone, claim compliance (no unsubstantiated health claims), and CTA clarity. Sign off in the brief doc before moving to production. Do NOT generate voice until the script is locked — re-generating wastes credits.
    ⚠️ Indian FSSAI rules: Do not make disease-prevention or cure claims (e.g., "cures diabetes"). Stick to: "high protein," "natural ingredients," "no added sugar." When in doubt, remove it.
Phase 2
2
Voice Generation — ElevenLabs
Script → Studio → Tweak → Export MP3
10–20 min per video
  • 1
    Open ElevenLabs Studio and select your voice clone
    Go to elevenlabs.io/studio → New Project → Select your saved Trulo voice clone from the voice library. Set the model to Multilingual V3 for best Indian English output.
    Voice: Trulo — [Name] (Indian EN) ← your saved clone
    Model: Eleven Multilingual v3
    Output format: MP3, 192 kbps
  • 2
    Paste the approved script and add SSML pauses
    Paste the script. Add manual pauses using ElevenLabs' pause markers to ensure natural breathing between sections. This prevents the avatar from appearing to speak without pause for 60 seconds straight.
    # SSML pause markers in ElevenLabs
    Short pause (comma equivalent): <break time="0.3s"/>
    Sentence pause: <break time="0.6s"/>
    Section transition: <break time="0.9s"/>
    # Or use ellipsis (...) in the text — ElevenLabs reads these as pauses
    💡 Add a 0.5s pause right after the hook line. Viewers need a half-beat to register what they just heard before the next sentence lands.
  • 3
    Generate and review the voiceover
    Click Generate. Listen to the full output. Check for: natural Indian English stress patterns, correct product name pronunciation, no awkward pauses or rushed sections, and emotional tone matching the script's intent.
    ✅ Pass: Sounds like a real person. Warm, confident, clear. No robotic cadence.
    ✅ Pass: Product names and brand name pronounced correctly.
    ✅ Pass: Total duration matches target length (±5 sec).
    ⚠️ If pronunciation is off for a word, use ElevenLabs' phonetic spelling override or the built-in pronunciation dictionary. For "Trulo" enter troo-loh.
  • 4
    Export the final audio
    Download → MP3, 192 kbps. Filename convention: trulo_[content-type]_[YYYYMMDD]_v1.mp3 — e.g., trulo_product-explainer_20260525_v1.mp3. Save to the project folder. This file goes directly into HeyGen in the next phase.
Phase 3
3
Avatar Production — HeyGen
Audio upload → Lip-sync → Background → Captions → Export
15–25 min per video
  • 1
    Open HeyGen and load your Brand Template
    Log in to app.heygen.com → Video Studio → Open your saved Trulo Brand Template. This pre-loads the correct aspect ratio, background, avatar position, and caption settings.
  • 2
    Select your avatar
    In the avatar panel, select your Trulo custom avatar (or your chosen stock avatar). Confirm the pose/framing looks correct in the preview. Use Avatar IV or V model — do not use older models.
    💡 For product-close-up content, use the "upper body" framing variant — it allows more gestures and feels more dynamic than a tight head shot.
  • 3
    Upload the ElevenLabs audio and disable HeyGen's built-in voice
    In the Audio panel → Upload Audio → select your exported MP3 from ElevenLabs. Important: Make sure HeyGen's own voice generation is set to "None / Upload Audio" — do not let HeyGen generate or blend its own voice. The lip-sync will now be driven entirely by your ElevenLabs audio.
    ⚠️ This is the most critical step. If you accidentally leave HeyGen voice on, it will generate a different voice and your Indian English accent will be replaced. Always verify the audio source in the preview before rendering.
  • 4
    Preview lip-sync accuracy
    Use HeyGen's preview function to watch a 5–10 second clip of the avatar speaking your audio. Verify lip movements match the words — especially on Indian English vowel sounds (aa, ai, o). If sync looks off, try re-uploading the audio at a slightly higher volume (normalise to -3dB).
    ✅ Pass: Lips close on stop consonants (p, b, m). Open on wide vowels (aa, a). No half-second lag between audio and lip movement.
  • 5
    Set background and visual elements
    Apply the background from your brand template. For social content, also consider:
    Background options:
    1. Solid brand colour (simplest, no distraction)
    2. Blurred kitchen / food scene (warm, on-brand)
    3. Gradient (off-white to warm cream — Trulo palette)
    Overlays to add:
    • Trulo logo watermark (top-right corner, 60% opacity)
    • Product name text card (bottom-left, if product explainer)
    • CTA banner in last 5 seconds (e.g. "trulofoods.com")
  • 6
    Enable and style auto-captions
    Captions → Auto-generate from audio → Review for any mis-transcriptions (product names, "Trulo") → Correct manually if needed. Style: white text, bold, with a subtle dark background for readability on any feed background.
    Caption style: Bold white, 36–42px, bottom third
    Background: Semi-transparent dark pill
    Max words per line: 5–6 words
    Corrections to always make: "trulo" → "Trulo", any product names
    💡 ~85% of Instagram Reels are watched without sound. Captions are not optional — they are the primary read path for most viewers.
  • 7
    Render and download the final video
    Click Render → 1080p → MP4. Rendering takes 2–5 minutes per minute of video. When done, download and filename using the convention: trulo_[type]_[platform]_[YYYYMMDD]_v1.mp4
    # File naming examples
    trulo_product-explainer_reels_20260525_v1.mp4
    trulo_ingredient-story_shorts_20260601_v1.mp4
    trulo_brand-story_youtube_20260610_v1.mp4
Phase 4
4
Post-Production & Publishing
QA review → Optional trim → Platform upload → Copy
10–20 min per video
  • 1
    Run the QA checklist (next section)
    Watch the full video once end-to-end on a mobile screen. Use the QA checklist on the next section of this SOP. If anything fails, go back to HeyGen (for visual/sync issues) or ElevenLabs (for audio issues) and re-generate only the affected part.
  • 2
    Optional: Light trim in CapCut or InShot
    HeyGen doesn't support trim edits after rendering. If you need to cut a pause at the start/end, or add a product B-roll cutaway, import the HeyGen MP4 into CapCut (free) and make the edit. Keep HeyGen output as the primary talking-head layer — don't recolour or filter the avatar's skin tone.
    💡 One common edit: add a 0.5s static product shot at the very end, after the CTA, to let the image "land" before the video loops.
  • 3
    Write platform copy before uploading
    Prepare caption copy, hashtags, and alt-text in a doc before opening the platform — reduces distracted ad-libbing. Use this structure:
    Hook lineFirst line mirrors the video hook — this is what shows in the feed before "more"
    Body (2–3 lines)Brief expansion of the video's key point. Human tone.
    CTASingle action: "Link in bio" / "DM 'TRULO' for 10% off"
    Hashtags5–8 relevant tags. Mix broad (#healthyfood) + niche (#cleaneating) + brand (#TruloFoods)
  • 4
    Upload to platform
    Instagram Reels: 9:16, 1080×1920, MP4, max 90 sec for Reels. Add cover thumbnail.
    YouTube Shorts: 9:16, 1080×1920, MP4, title ≤ 100 chars, include #Shorts in title.
    YouTube long-form: 16:9, 1920×1080, MP4. Upload custom thumbnail.
    LinkedIn: Square (1:1) or 16:9. Add captions file or rely on auto-captions.
  • 5
    Archive the project files
    Save to a shared drive folder named Trulo / Avatar Videos / [YYYY-MM] / [Video Name] containing: the approved script doc, ElevenLabs MP3, HeyGen MP4, and published post URL. This makes repurposing and revision easy.
Quality Gate

QA Checklist

Run before every publish. Watch the video on a phone at full screen — the final viewer experience. If any box is unchecked, fix it before publishing.

How to use this checklist

Print this page or copy it into Notion. One person runs through every item for each video. Fail = go back and fix. Every video must clear all items before it goes live.

🎙️ Audio
  • Voice sounds like natural Indian English — no American vowel shift or robotic cadenceListen on earphones, not speakers
  • Product name and brand name ("Trulo") pronounced correctly
  • Audio is clean — no background noise, hiss, or clipping
  • Pacing feels natural — pauses between sentences, not rushed
  • Volume is consistent throughout — no sudden loud or quiet sections
👁️ Visual / Avatar
  • Lip-sync is accurate — lips open/close matches audio consonants and vowelsCheck at 1–2 specific words: "Trulo", "natural", "energy"
  • No visual glitches — no stuttering avatar frame, no frozen face, no pixelation
  • Avatar framing is correct — head centred, not cut off at top or side
  • Background looks clean and on-brand
  • Trulo logo watermark is visible but not intrusive
📝 Captions
  • Captions are present and auto-synced throughout the entire video
  • All product names, brand names spelled correctly in captionsCommon error: "trulo" → fix to "Trulo"
  • Caption text is readable on both light and dark backgrounds
  • No caption overlaps with logo or CTA overlay
📋 Content
  • Hook lands in first 3 seconds — compelling reason to keep watching
  • Script matches approved version — no unreviewd ad-libs
  • No unsubstantiated health claims (FSSAI compliance)
  • CTA is clear and appears in the final 5 seconds
  • Video duration matches target length (±5 sec)
📐 Technical
  • Exported at 1080p — not 720p (free-tier default)
  • Aspect ratio matches target platform (9:16 for Reels/Shorts; 16:9 for YouTube)
  • No HeyGen watermark present (confirm Creator plan is active)
  • File size is reasonable for upload (<500 MB for a 60-sec video)

The golden rule of this workflow

Always generate audio in ElevenLabs first. Always upload that audio to HeyGen. Never use HeyGen's own voice for Indian English. This single discipline is what keeps the accent, tone, and brand voice consistent across every video you produce.
