## TIER 1 — PRIMARY STYLE REFERENCE

---

### ★ 1. Kurzgesagt – In a Nutshell — THE STYLE STANDARD
**Type:** YouTube animation studio (Munich) — 24M+ subscribers, 310+ videos
**Style:** Flat semi-realistic illustration; bold warm saturated fills; rounded organic forms; colored outer glow on active elements; deep dark backgrounds; personality-driven biological entities
**This is Trulo's locked style.** Every organ, molecule, scene, and process diagram should look like it could appear in a Kurzgesagt biology video.

**What defines Kurzgesagt biology:**
- Deep dark background (blue-black or warm dark) — not clinical white
- Bold flat fills, max 3 tonal steps — not photorealistic gradients
- Rounded forms everywhere — organs are recognizable but edges are eased
- Colored glow on active/healthy elements — the primary "alive" indicator
- Molecules and cells have personality — they feel purposeful, not passive
- Clean integrated typography — Poppins-style, color-matched, never crowded

**Relevant content:**
- How The Immune System Works: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXfEK8G8CUI — organ glow, cell personality, dark background
- Ozempic/GLP-1 video: search on channel — glucose metabolism in Kurzgesagt style
- Cells protein language video: https://thekidshouldseethis.com/post/cells-complex-language-kurzgesagt-video — molecule particle systems
- Style frames on Dribbble: https://dribbble.com/tags/kurzgesagt — detailed design reference
- Pinterest art style: https://www.pinterest.com/ideas/kurzgesagt-art-style/934861879895/ — full color palette

**How to adapt for Trulo:** Replace Kurzgesagt's typical blue/teal/purple palette with Trulo's warm saffron/terracotta/linen. Keep every structural rule. The warmth is the only thing that changes.

### 0. WEHI.TV — Walter & Eliza Hall Institute ⭐ WATCH FOR CONTENT ACCURACY
**Type:** Scientific research institute biomedical animation (Melbourne, Australia)
**Lead animator:** Dr Drew Berry — [TED Talk: Animations of unseeable biology](https://www.ted.com/talks/drew_berry_animations_of_unseeable_biology)
**Style:** Cinematic semi-realistic molecular animation — *not* Trulo's style, but biologically the most accurate depiction of insulin, beta cells, and glucose receptors that exists.
**How to use:** Watch to understand *what* happens biologically (insulin receptor binding, beta cell release, inflammation cascade). Then illustrate that content in Kurzgesagt style. WEHI shows the truth; Kurzgesagt shows how to communicate it beautifully.
**Diabetes animations — watch all of these:**
- Insulin Receptor and Type 2 Diabetes: https://www.wehi.edu.au/wehi-tv/insulin-receptor-and-type-2-diabetes/
- Insulin Production and Type 1 Diabetes: https://www.wehi.edu.au/wehi-tv/insulin-production-and-type-1-diabetes/
- Inflammation and Type 2 Diabetes: https://www.wehi.edu.au/wehi-tv/inflammation-and-type-2-diabetes/
- Full diabetes library: https://www.wehi.edu.au/topic/diabetes/
**Borrow for Trulo:** Everything. Warm amber glow. How insulin molecules look when they bind to receptors. How beta cells look when they release insulin. The "cosmic interior" feel.

### 1. Kurzgesagt – In a Nutshell
**Type:** YouTube animation studio (Munich)
**Subscribers:** 24M+ | 310+ videos
**Style:** Flat-to-semi-flat illustration; bold color fills; rounded characters; playful but scientifically grounded
**What makes it work:** They take brutally complex biology (immune system, CRISPR, DNA) and make it feel like you're visiting another universe — not a hospital. Rich color palettes, smooth transitions, objects that feel *alive*.
**Relevant content:**

- "Ozempic/GLP-1" — glucose metabolism concepts with their trademark rounded forms
- "Type 1 Diabetes" — autoimmune pathway visualized in accessible style
- Immune System 3-part series — shows how biological systems interact beautifully
**Key links:**
- Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@kurzgesagt
- Immune system video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXfEK8G8CUI
- CRISPR animation: https://innovativegenomics.org/multimedia-library/kurzgesagt-video-animates-crispr/
- Cells language video: https://thekidshouldseethis.com/post/cells-complex-language-kurzgesagt-video
**Borrow for Trulo:** Color-coding of biological systems; smooth camera moves into organs; making molecular processes feel magical not clinical. Their warm-orange palette maps to Trulo's saffron.

### 2. XVIVO Scientific Animation — "The Inner Life of the Cell"
**Type:** Professional medical animation studio (Wethersfield, CT)
**Founded:** 2000 by David Bolinsky (former Yale Medical Illustrator)
**Style:** High-fidelity 3D; semi-realistic molecular visualization; cinematic lighting; intentionally beautiful even at cellular scale
**Flagship work:** *The Inner Life of the Cell* (2006, Harvard) — 8.5 min showing a white blood cell during inflammation. 3.5M+ views. This is the benchmark for "biology as cinema."
**What makes it work:** Uses actual biophysical data. Proteins "jitter" with realistic motion. The environment feels vast and cosmic — not a medical textbook.
**Relevant content:**
- Inner Life of the Cell: https://xvivo.com/examples/the-inner-life-of-the-cell/
- Protein Packing: https://xvivo.com/examples/the-inner-life-of-the-cell-protein-packing-2/
- Full case study: https://xvivo.com/case-studies/the-inner-life-of-the-cell-series/
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wJyUtbn0O5Y
**Borrow for Trulo:** The "cosmos inside the body" feeling. Glowing particles. How glucose molecules could behave like XVIVO's protein particles — drifting, purposeful, elegant.
