## SECTION 4 — SPECIFIC CONTENT TO SCREENSHOT/SAVE

### From Kurzgesagt — Frame targets
- Any frame showing organs inside a body (warm palette, semi-transparent)
- Any frame showing molecule particles flowing through a system
- Any frame showing "healthy vs. unhealthy" state comparison
- The color palette used in their biology series (warm oranges + blues)

### From XVIVO Inner Life of the Cell — Frame targets
- 0:40–1:20 — The "rolling" scene (molecular motion elegance)
- 2:00–2:40 — Inside the cell (glowing environment)
- 4:00–5:00 — Motor proteins walking on microtubules (motion inspiration)
- Any frame where particles stream/flow (glucose equivalent visualization)

### From Nucleus Medical Type 2 Diabetes — Frame targets
- The pancreas releasing insulin (organ close-up style)
- The bloodstream glucose flooding scene
- The cell receptor/insulin binding scene
- The "before/after" healthy vs. resistant pancreas

### From Glucose Goddess Instagram
- Her glucose spike curve graphics (data visualization style)
- Before/after meal glucose graphs
- Any infographic style content
